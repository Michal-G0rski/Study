## Week 4 — 23 to 29 November

Split: 2.5 h Networking review, 12.5 h Python project.

Day | Work through
:-- | :--
Mon 23 | Networking review, applied — your first hostile capture: one recent traffic exercise from [malware-traffic-analysis.net](https://www.malware-traffic-analysis.net/) (the site says start from the newest). Find the infected host, the suspicious domains and the odd User-Agent from the PCAP alone; CIDR, TCP flags, DNS messages and ARP are what you will be reading. The answer key only after 30 stuck minutes. Open the PCAP in Wireshark only — don't export files out of it on your own machine; these archives carry real malware traffic.
