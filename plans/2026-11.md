# November 2026 — Stage 1, Foundations

Month 2 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to write, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Five study weeks this month, because 30 November is a Monday.

## Sources in use this month

- [Modern C](https://gustedt.gitlabpages.inria.fr/modern-c/) — the free C23 edition, revision of 15 October 2024. **This month finishes it.** Chapter and section numbers come from that PDF and its own table of contents; if your copy numbers differently, the topic names are the contract.
- [Mathematics for Computer Science](https://openlearninglibrary.mit.edu/courses/course-v1:OCW+6.042J+2T2019/about) — unit 1 to its end, at the same 5 h/week as October.
- [Beej's Guide to C Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgc/html/split/) — second-pass reading on Saturdays. Never the first pass on anything.

Missing Semester is finished and does not appear. Rust does not start in Stage 1.
