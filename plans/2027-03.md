# March 2027 — Sentinel, ATT&CK, and the first incident

Month 6 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Five weeks, seventy-two and a half hours. Easter Monday falls on 29 March and is the only day out; it lands on the first day of week 5, which is the lightest week of the month for exactly that reason.

The whole month is the SOC operations track. Windows and AD fundamentals are in place from February; every hour now goes into the tool the job runs on — **a SIEM** — and the language it speaks. Three threads: the [Microsoft Sentinel learning path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/sc-200-configure-azure-sentinel-environment/) (workspace, connectors, KQL, detections), **MITRE ATT&CK** as vocabulary rather than reading, and the first [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io/) incident handled end to end. The [TryHackMe](https://www.tryhackme.com/) SOC Level 1 path runs alongside at 3–5 hours/week. And [SC-200](../README.md#certifications) gets **booked** this month, with a date in mid-to-late April — the commitment device, before you feel ready.

## Sources in use this month

- [Microsoft Sentinel learning path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/sc-200-configure-azure-sentinel-environment/) — workspace, data sources, querying, detections. Free, and the SIEM vocabulary a triage seat runs on.
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) — the framework, as vocabulary. Know the tactics cold; recognize technique families on sight.
- [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io/) — free blue-team labs: alerts arrive, you triage them. The first full incident is this month's deliverable.
- [TryHackMe](https://www.tryhackme.com/) — the SOC Level 1 path, 3–5 hours/week, alongside everything else.

> A SIEM is learned in the fingers, not the eyes. Every module in the Sentinel path gets its queries written by you, not pasted. The same rule the C programs always had.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Three things specific to this month:

- **The SIEM hours are the protected ones.** SC-200 is April and the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) is June, and both stand on this.
- **Book the exam before you feel ready.** The date is the commitment device; the gap list is what April is for.
- **The incident report is a deliverable, not a bonus.** It is the portfolio piece that proves the triage vocabulary is not just vocabulary.

## Where April picks up

Month 7. The [SC-200](../README.md#certifications) exam happens: the gap list closed, the Defender XDR and Defender for Cloud modules covered, practice assessments sat under a clock, and then the exam itself. The last week of April is the exam's tail and its accounting, not more preparation.
