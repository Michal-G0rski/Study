# March 2027 — Stage 2, Sentinel Finishes & MITRE ATT&CK Begins

Month 6 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Four clean weeks, 60 hours. March 2027 begins on a Monday and has no public holidays on a study day this early (Easter falls in April this year).

The first three weeks finish the Sentinel/KQL block started in [February](2027-02.md) — analytics rules, hunting, workbooks, the first LetsDefend incident end-to-end. The fourth week opens MITRE ATT&CK. Same rule as February: **everything that has to survive past this month lives in Wazuh, not in the Learn sandbox** — the sandbox is for learning the SC-200 syllabus, Wazuh is for the portfolio.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **No personal Azure resource is ever created.** Everything Sentinel-side stays inside the free Learn sandbox; everything that has to persist goes into Wazuh instead.
- The detection library is the deliverable, not the individual rules. A rule that only exists in one engine is half the point of this month.
- If a sandbox module resets or expires mid-week, that is expected — screenshot and document what you built before it goes, then rebuild the parts that matter in Wazuh.
