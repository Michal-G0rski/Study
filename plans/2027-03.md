# March 2027 — the Academy at full weight, and the track closes

Month 6 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Five weeks, seventy-two and a half hours. Easter Monday falls on 29 March and is the only day out; it lands on the first day of week 5, which is the lightest week of the month for exactly that reason.

The whole month is the [Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security). Networking is finished, mathematics is finished, and every hour the plan has goes into closing the beginner-to-intermediate track: authentication, access control, information disclosure, business logic — and the track **closes** at the end of the month, which is what makes April the eJPT month.

## Sources in use this month

- [Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security) — authentication (carried from February), access control, information disclosure, and business logic vulnerabilities. [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) alongside, as context.
- [eJPT](https://ine.com/security/certifications/ejpt-certification/) — no study hours this month. The exam gets **booked**, which is the whole point of the entry.

> A topic is closed when its labs are solved, not when its pages are read. The Academy's own accounting is the lab list; use it.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Three things specific to this month:

- **The track has to close this month.** eJPT is April and the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) is June, and both stand on this. If a week is short, cut depth on the last topic's labs, never the closure.
- **A lab you read the solution for is not a lab you solved.** Same rule as February, and it is the rule the accounting in week 5 exists to enforce.
- **Book the exam before you feel ready.** The date is the commitment device; the gap list is what April is for.

## What the track leaves open

The Academy has more topics than five months can close, and it always did. **File upload and SSRF close in April, inside eJPT preparation, because the eJPT syllabus assumes them.** Everything else — XXE, XSS, CSRF, insecure deserialization, web cache poisoning and deception, GraphQL, NoSQL injection, race conditions, WebSockets, clickjacking, CORS, web LLM attacks, API testing — is left as backlog for when a posting asks. They are the advanced and specialist half of the Academy and none of them is on the path to [the roles this plan targets at the checkpoint](../README.md#target-roles).

## Where April picks up

Month 7. The [eJPT](../README.md#certifications) exam happens: a gap list, the gaps closed, file upload and SSRF folded in as exam-relevant topics, a timed dry run, and then the exam itself. The last week of April is the exam, not the preparation for it.
