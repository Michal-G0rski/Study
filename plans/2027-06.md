## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The exam week is the exam week.** Do not schedule practice or reading into it as a hedge. Two half-done things is the failure mode here.
- **If the exam is failed, it is failed and rebooked, and the plan does not change.** The [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) leans on the certificate, but it stands on the incident reports and the detection library just as much. A retake costs days, not the month.
- **The Purple-Team build starts on schedule regardless of the exam result.** Waiting for a retake to build confidence first just shortens the build phase later.
