# June 2027 — the checkpoint, and the stack overwritten

Month 9 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Four clean weeks, sixty hours, no holidays.

The [employability checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--months-910) opens this month, and it is the one part of this plan that is not about learning. The main plan is blunt about why: *"Carrying on studying is the comfortable option, not the right one."* Applications are a deliverable in June and July exactly like a lab is.

On the main track: CS:APP chapter 7 and the **Attack Lab**. In May you learned to read a stack frame. This month you overwrite one.

## Sources in use this month

- [Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~213/) — chapter 7, linking, and the **Attack Lab** from the [self-study labs](http://csapp.cs.cmu.edu/3e/labs.html).
- [picoCTF / CyLab Security Academy](https://cylabacademy.org) — opens here, at the five hours a week PortSwigger has held since February. Start with binary exploitation and reverse engineering; those are the categories your last four months point at.
- The [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--months-910) itself, and the [market snapshot](../README.md#market-snapshot-september-2026) from September 2026, which exists to be re-measured.

> The Attack Lab gives you two binaries. One is vulnerable to code injection; the other has the protections that make code injection useless, and has to be attacked with return-oriented programming instead — using fragments of the program's own code as the payload. That second half is the technique Stage 4 spends four months on, and this is where you meet it.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Three things specific to this month:

- **Applications do not slip.** They are the one item here with an outside clock on them, and the whole argument for the checkpoint is arithmetic: getting hired at month 9 turns the remaining nine months from unpaid study into paid study. A week of applications postponed is a week of that postponed.
- **Do not wait until you feel ready.** You will not. The main plan anticipated this and wrote the instruction down on purpose.
- **The Attack Lab is the prerequisite for Stage 4**, in the same way the Bomb Lab was the prerequisite for reading disassembly. If the reading slips, the reading slips.
