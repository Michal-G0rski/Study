# June 2027 — Stage 2, SC-200 Exam & Purple-Team Begins

Month 9 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Three weeks, 45 hours. No public holidays land on a study day this month. The first two weeks finish the exam-prep block that started in [May](2027-05.md) and sit the SC-200 exam; the third opens the Purple-Team project — the last major piece of Stage 2 before the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) in August.

## Week 3 — 21 to 26 June

The Purple-Team project begins. Split: all on standing up and instrumenting the target app.

Day | Work through
:-- | :--
Mon 21 | Stand the chosen app up locally (Docker): get it running, understand its auth and session flow, confirm you can reach every route.
Tue 22 | Map the security-relevant surface: registration, login, password handling, session lifecycle, cookies — note what the app does well and what is intentionally weak.
Wed 23 | Instrument logging: make sure every login, failed login and privileged action is logged with the fields a detection will need (user, IP, UA, timestamp, outcome, correlation ID) — add structured logging where the app lacks it.
Thu 24 | Understand the data layer: schema, queries, where input reaches the database — the parts an injection attack in July will target, and what a detection would see.
Fri 25 | Confirm at least two user-facing features that write data (notes, uploads, messages) work and log; add one small feature only if the app lacks a second write path.
Sat 26 | Consolidate: what runs, what logs cleanly, what next week's instrumentation must finish before the freeze.

**Done by Sunday**
- [ ] Target app running locally and its auth & session flow understood
- [ ] Security event logging instrumented, emitting detection-ready fields
- [ ] Data layer understood and at least two logging-covered write paths working
- [ ] Six commits

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The exam week is the exam week.** Do not schedule practice or reading into it as a hedge. Two half-done things is the failure mode here.
- **If the exam is failed, it is failed and rebooked, and the plan does not change.** The [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) leans on the certificate, but it stands on the incident reports and the detection library just as much. A retake costs days, not the month — and a second full exam fee, which is why May's practice assessments are not optional.
- **The Purple-Team instrumentation starts on schedule regardless of the exam result.** Waiting for a retake to build confidence first just shortens the work later.
