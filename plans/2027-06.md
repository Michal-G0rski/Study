# June 2027 — Stage 2, Checkpoint Opens

Month 9 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Four weeks, 60 hours. 6 June falls on a Sunday; no public holidays land on study days.

Two things, and the second is the one the whole plan was built to reach. First, **triage practice and the interview vocabulary**: the SOC skills are finished since May — June\'s job is to make them perform under questioning, not to add new ones. Second, the [**employability checkpoint**](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) opens: CV, public profile, the market re-measured, and the first applications actually sent — **led by SOC / Detection Engineer (L1)**, with junior QA as the measured fallback.

After this month there is no more fixed schedule. The deep low-level curriculum — architecture, reverse engineering, exploitation, embedded work, and the PortSwigger track and eJPT with it — is the [backlog](../README.md#after-the-checkpoint), picked up in whatever order the job you land demands.

## Sources in use this month

- The artifacts you already own: the blog (five posts), the SC-200 certificate, the LetsDefend incident reports, the Purple-Team project with its KQL/Sigma detections. June adds no new material; it points the existing material at employers.
- The [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) itself, and the [market snapshot](../README.md#market-snapshot-september-2026) from September 2026, which exists to be re-measured.
- Sample alerts for triage practice: the Sentinel community content, LetsDefend\'s free alerts, or any public alert set.

> The job of an interview is to make you explain your own artifacts under pressure. Every practice answer this month is built from something you actually did — never from a script.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The applications go out in June, whatever state the artifacts are in.** Perfection is the comfortable option; the market is the other one.
- **The re-measurement decides the ordering, not hope.** Twenty-four junior security openings was the September count; the June count is the number that matters now.
- **Do not open a third program of study.** If something is missing for a posting, that is what the [backlog](../README.md#after-the-checkpoint) and the job itself are for.

What comes next is the [backlog](../README.md#after-the-checkpoint), driven by the job you land — or, if nothing has landed yet, by the default: [CS:APP with the labs](../README.md#computer-architecture-and-systems), the single most-leveraged item on the shelf, worked at whatever pace the applications leave room for. The aim of the fixed schedule was a job and a portfolio. The backlog is the career.
