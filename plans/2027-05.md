# May 2027 — Stage 2, SC-200 Exam Prep Begins: Defender XDR & Cloud

Month 8 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Five weeks, 70 hours. Two public holidays land on study days — 3 May (week 1) and 27 May (week 4) — so those weeks run at 12.5 h, the other three at 15 h. This is the first half of a seven-week SC-200 exam-prep block; the second half, including the exam itself, is in [June](2027-06.md).

**The default is still card-free — but this is the one month where hands-on is worth chasing.** Defender XDR, Defender for Cloud and Entra ID have no permanently-free lab, so the base plan studies them from [Microsoft Learn's free modules and product docs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) as concept, and closes as much of that gap as it can for free: **[Microsoft Applied Skills](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/browse/?credential_types=applied%20skills)** assessments (Sentinel and Defender tracks run in a real hosted lab in the browser), plus the demo environment, ADX, Wazuh, LetsDefend and the practice assessments. The **optional** extra, and the one place a card appears in the whole plan: an [Azure free trial](https://azure.microsoft.com/free/) opened for this billing month only, with a **hard spending limit set to 0 / a budget alert**, the free Sentinel ingestion allowance, and **the tenant and resource group deleted the day the exam is done** ([the bounded-cost note](../README.md#certifications)). Take it if you want tenant-UI familiarity; skip it and nothing in the exam prep breaks. What is *not* optional: before building this month's day plan, re-read the note below — Microsoft's portal and objective domains move.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **If you open the optional Azure trial, it stays bounded — spending limit at 0, and torn down the day the exam is done.** The default is still to learn Defender and Entra from the free modules, docs and Applied Skills; the trial is a convenience for tenant-UI familiarity, never an open-ended resource left running. No other billable account is touched, and the core hands-on hours stay on KQL (demo environment + ADX), Wazuh, LetsDefend and the practice assessments.
- The SIEM/exam-prep hours are protected. The exam sits in June and the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) in August; both stand on this.
- If a practice assessment score is bad, that is the point of taking it in May and not the week before the exam.
