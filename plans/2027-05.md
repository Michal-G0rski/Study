# May 2027 — Stage 2, SC-200 Exam Prep Begins: Defender XDR & Cloud

Month 8 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Five weeks, 70 hours. Two public holidays land on study days — 3 May (week 1) and 27 May (week 4) — so those weeks run at 12.5 h, the other three at 15 h. This is the first half of a seven-week SC-200 exam-prep block; the second half, including the exam itself, is in [June](2027-06.md).

**Still no personal Azure subscription.** Defender XDR, Defender for Cloud and Entra ID are studied from [Microsoft Learn's free modules and Microsoft's product documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) — read, watched and worked through as concept, since these products have no free hands-on lab. That is a deliberate, accepted gap: fully operating a live Defender/Entra tenant needs a paid subscription this plan refuses. Nothing this month can generate a bill, because nothing this month touches an account that can be billed — the hands-on hours stay on KQL in the demo environment, Wazuh, LetsDefend and the practice assessments.

- [SC-200 study paths on Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) — official, free modules (concept; read + knowledge checks, no live tenant)

> The exam's three legs: Sentinel (Feb/March's ground), Defender XDR, and Defender for Cloud, with Entra ID woven through all three. Seven weeks across May and June instead of the four the old version of this plan gave it — that compression was the single sharpest density risk anyone flagged.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **No personal Azure resource is ever created.** Defender and Entra are learned from the free modules and docs; where the syllabus wants a live tenant, it is studied as concept, not operated — an accepted gap. The hands-on hours stay on KQL (demo environment), Wazuh, LetsDefend and the practice assessments.
- The SIEM/exam-prep hours are protected. The exam sits in June and the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) in August; both stand on this.
- If a practice assessment score is bad, that is the point of taking it in May and not the week before the exam.
