# May 2027 — Stage 2, SC-200 Exam Prep Begins: Defender XDR & Cloud

Month 8 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Five weeks, 70 hours. Two public holidays land on study days — 3 May (week 1) and 27 May (week 4) — so those weeks run at 12.5 h, the other three at 15 h. This is the first half of a seven-week SC-200 exam-prep block; the second half, including the exam itself, is in [June](2027-06.md).

**Still no personal Azure subscription.** Defender XDR, Defender for Cloud and Entra ID are all covered by [Microsoft Learn's free, pre-provisioned sandbox labs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) — every hands-on exercise in this block runs there. Nothing in this month can generate a bill, because nothing in this month touches an account that can be billed.

## Sources in use this month

- [SC-200 study paths on Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) — official, free, sandbox-based preparation
- Microsoft Defender XDR (formerly Microsoft 365 Defender) — sandbox
- Microsoft Defender for Cloud — sandbox
- [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io/) — incident #3
- [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/) — SOC Level 1 path continued at 3–5 h/week

> The exam's three legs: Sentinel (Feb/March's ground), Defender XDR, and Defender for Cloud, with Entra ID woven through all three. Seven weeks across May and June instead of the four the old version of this plan gave it — that compression was the single sharpest density risk anyone flagged.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **No personal Azure resource is ever created.** Every hands-on exercise this month runs in the free Learn sandbox. If a module insists on a personal subscription, skip it — the sandbox covers the syllabus.
- The SIEM/exam-prep hours are protected. The exam sits in June and the [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-11) in August; both stand on this.
- If a practice assessment score is bad, that is the point of taking it in May and not the week before the exam.
