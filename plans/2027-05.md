# May 2027 — Stage 2, Purple-Team Project: Build → Attack → Detect

Month 8 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Five weeks, 70 hours. Two public holidays on study days — 3 May (week 1) and 27 May (week 4) — so those weeks run at 12.5 h, the other three at 15 h.

## Sources in use this month

- Your own application — small, real, yours. Any stack; the point is the shapes real bugs live in, not the framework.
- Sysmon (SwiftOnSecurity or Olaf Hartong config) — already installed from December.
- Your Sentinel workspace + KQL detection library from February–March.
- [Sigma](https://github.com/SigmaHQ/sigma) specification and `sigmac` compiler.

> Write the application the way you would if you were not about to attack it — and log like someone who will have to detect it. A planted bug teaches nothing; an unplanted one that survives to week 3 is the whole deliverable.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The app has to freeze on schedule.** Week 3 cannot attack what week 3 is still building. If the build runs long, cut features, never the freeze.
- **Do not plant bugs.** The writeup is honest or it is worthless — and finding none is a real result to write up too.
- **The detections are the deliverable, not the bugs.** Anyone can break a web app; the junior a SOC wants is the one who can write the rule that catches it.
