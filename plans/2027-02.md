# February 2027 — the book finishes, Windows opens

Month 5 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

February 2027 begins on a Monday and ends on a Sunday. Four clean weeks, no holidays, sixty hours — the first month since November with nothing taken out of it.

Two things run this month. **Networking closes**: Kurose chapters 7 and 8, from the book — the lecture site has never published material for either. And **Windows opens**: the [September 2026 decision](../README.md#summary) aims the plan at SOC / Detection Engineer (L1), and a triage seat lives in Windows — its processes, its services, its directory, its event logs — long before it lives in anything else. The hours the PortSwigger Academy used to take go there now; the Academy itself is [backlog](../README.md#after-the-checkpoint).

## Sources in use this month

- [Computer Networking: a Top-Down Approach](https://gaia.cs.umass.edu/kurose_ross/online_lectures.htm) — chapters 7 and 8, **from the book**. Nothing was ever filmed for either, so there is nothing to watch; the topics below are the contract, not section numbers.
- [TryHackMe](https://www.tryhackme.com/) — the Windows rooms, hands-on: Windows Fundamentals 1, 2 and 3, then Active Directory Basics. Free-tier access is enough; a room you watched is not a room you did.
- A Windows machine you can break — a VM you own, snapshots taken first. The rooms explain; your own VM is where the month's notes actually get written.

> Why Windows, and why now: the [market snapshot](../README.md#market-snapshot-september-2026) put SOC L1 first because it is the one door in security with a measured junior queue. Read a handful of junior SOC postings and count the Windows terms. The plan follows the postings.
