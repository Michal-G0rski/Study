# October 2026 — Stage 1, Foundations

Month 1 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

This file is scope only — what to read, what to write, what has to exist by Sunday. Environment, tooling and session setup are deliberately not here.

## Sources in use this month

- [Modern C](https://gustedt.gitlabpages.inria.fr/modern-c/) — the free C23 edition, revision of 15 October 2024. Chapter numbers below come from that PDF's own table of contents. **If your copy numbers differently, the topic names are the contract, not the numbers** — the author states that the print edition differs in numbering and layout.
- [The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) — lectures 1–10.
- [Beej's Guide to C Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgc/html/split/) — second-pass reading only, never the first pass.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- Modern C never slips two days running. It is the one prerequisite in this plan with no workaround.
- If something has to go it is the Missing Semester lecture, then the second-pass reading. Never the programs.
- A missed day moves to Sunday, at most once a month.
- Do not run ahead. A chapter read on Tuesday that was scheduled for Thursday gets read instead of done.
