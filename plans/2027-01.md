## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- The three attacks (Kerberoasting, AS-REP, DCSync) are the month. Lectures can slip; the labs cannot.
- Stage 1 review is not a formality and does not move. Four months is exactly long enough to forget what you did in the first one.
- **Week 5 stays a consolidation week.** If it's tempting to start the Sentinel sandbox early, don't — February opens it properly, and running ahead here just means re-reading the same modules twice.
