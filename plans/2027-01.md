# January 2027 — Stage 1 ends, Stage 2 continues

Month 4 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to write, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Four full weeks, all fifteen hours. 6 January is a public holiday and is scheduled here as a study day anyway; if you take it, week 1 is the one week of the month with an obvious place to put it back — Sunday the 10th.

**Stage 1 ends on 31 January.** The last Saturday of the month is the review, and it is a deliverable like any other.

## Sources in use this month

- [Computer Networking: a Top-Down Approach, online lectures](https://gaia.cs.umass.edu/kurose_ross/online_lectures.htm) — the rest of chapter 3, then chapters 4, 5 and 6.
- [Beej on network programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/html/) — chapters 8 and 9, as reference rather than reading.

> **A gap in the lecture series, worth knowing before you plan around it.** The video pages cover chapters 1 to 4 completely. Chapter 5 has no video for 5.5 or 5.7, chapter 6 has videos only for 6.1 to 6.3, chapter 7 has no videos at all, and chapter 8 has no page. The site has said chapters 7 and 8 are "to follow" since 2021. Everything without a video in chapters 5 and 6 is scheduled below as reading; chapters 7 and 8 move to February and come out of the book.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Two things specific to this month:

- The three programs are the month. Lectures can slip a week; the DNS resolver and the traceroute cannot, because they are the first things you have written that construct a protocol rather than consume a library.
- The Stage 1 review is not a formality and does not move. Four months is exactly long enough to forget what you did in the first one.

## What Stage 1 leaves open, on purpose

- **Kurose chapters 7 and 8** — wireless and mobile networks, and network security. The lecture series has never covered them. They are read from the book in February.
- **Mathematics for Computer Science and Rust** — see [Extras](../README.md#extras-optional); picked up after the checkpoint or when a role asks.
