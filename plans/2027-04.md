# April 2027 — the first certificate

Month 7 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Four weeks, fifty-seven and a half hours. 1 May is a public holiday and falls on the Saturday that closes week 4 — the shortest week of the month is the exam week, which is the right way round.

One thing carries the month: the [eJPT](https://ine.com/security/certifications/ejpt-certification/) exam, booked in March with a date. The whole month is preparation, dry run, exam, and the honest writeup of whatever happened. It closes the certificate half of the employability track and gives the [month 9 checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) something to stand on besides writeups.

## Sources in use this month

- [eJPT](https://ine.com/security/certifications/ejpt-certification/) — the syllabus against your gap list, then the exam.
- [Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security) — file upload vulnerabilities and SSRF, folded in as exam-relevant topics, not as track extension.
- [picoCTF / CyLab Security Academy](https://cylabacademy.org) and [TryHackMe](https://www.tryhackme.com/) — open here as exam practice, 5 h/week.

> The eJPT assumes web attack surface, networking, and basic enumeration — all of which you have. What it asks that the Academy alone does not teach is hands-on time against practice targets: scanning, enumeration, footholds. That is what the practice hours this month are for.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Three things specific to this month:

- **The exam week is the exam week.** Do not schedule practice or reading into it as a hedge. Two half-done things is the failure mode here.
- **If the exam is failed, it is failed and rebooked, and the plan does not change.** The [checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) in June leans on the certificate, but it stands on the PortSwigger track and the writeups too. A retake costs days, not the month.
- **Nothing published this month, on purpose.** The writing month is May — the own-app build-and-break writeup is the artifact that matters.

## Where May picks up

Month 8. The own web application: designed, built, deployed, frozen — and then broken with the entire PortSwigger toolkit turned back on it. Both halves in your own hands, and the writeup published at the end of the month. It is the single clearest evidence for [Application Security Engineer](../README.md#target-roles) at the checkpoint, and it is the month the plan has been walking toward since February.
