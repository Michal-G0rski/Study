# April 2027 — the first certificate

Month 7 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Scope only — what to read, what to build, what has to exist by Sunday. Setup and tooling are deliberately not here. Standing rules are in [plans/README.md](README.md).

Four weeks, fifty-seven and a half hours. 1 May is a public holiday and falls on the Saturday that closes week 4 — the shortest week of the month is the exam's tail, which is the right way round.

One thing carries the month: the [SC-200](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) — Microsoft Security Operations Analyst — booked in March with a date. The whole month is the gap list from March closed, the parts of the syllabus the Sentinel path does not cover, practice under a clock, and then the exam itself. It gives the [month 9 checkpoint](../README.md#employability-checkpoint--month-9) a certificate that matches the door it aims at, and clears the HR filter that a portfolio alone cannot.

## Sources in use this month

- [SC-200 study paths](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/sc-200/) on Microsoft Learn — the official free preparation. Sentinel you already know from March; Defender XDR and Defender for Cloud you do not yet.
- [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io/) — one further incident, light, to keep the hands in practice during a theory-heavy month.
- [TryHackMe](https://www.tryhackme.com/) — the SOC Level 1 path, continued at 3–5 hours/week.

> The exam's three legs: Sentinel (March's ground), Microsoft Defender XDR, and Defender for Cloud. The second and third are new — do not let them arrive in exam week for the first time.

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

The [standing rules](README.md) apply. Three things specific to this month:

- **The exam week is the exam week.** Do not schedule practice or reading into it as a hedge. Two half-done things is the failure mode here.
- **If the exam is failed, it is failed and rebooked, and the plan does not change.** The checkpoint leans on the certificate, but it stands on the incident report and the detections as much. A retake costs days, not the month.
- **Nothing published this month, on purpose.** The writing month is May — the built-attacked-detected writeup is the artifact that matters.

## Where May picks up

Month 8. The own web application: designed, built, deployed, frozen — and then attacked, with the attacks captured in its logs and detections written for them. Build, attack, detect: the single clearest evidence for [SOC / Detection Engineer (L1)](../README.md#target-roles) at the checkpoint, and the month the plan has been walking toward since February.
