## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The exam week is the exam week.** Do not schedule practice or reading into it as a hedge. Two half-done things is the failure mode here.
- **If the exam is failed, it is failed and rebooked, and the plan does not change.** The checkpoint leans on the certificate, but it stands on the incident reports and detection library as much. A retake costs days, not the month.
- **Nothing published this month, on purpose.** The writing month is May — the Purple-Team writeup is the artifact that matters.
