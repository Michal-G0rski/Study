# April 2027 — Stage 2, ATT&CK Finishes, Phishing Triage & SC-200 Booked

Month 7 of [the plan](../README.md). **15 h/week: 2.5 h/day, Monday to Saturday, Sunday off.**

Five weeks, 70 hours. Easter Monday (29 March) opens week 1 as a holiday; the rest of the month has no public holidays on a study day. This month closes MITRE ATT&CK, finishes LetsDefend incident #2, adds a new phishing/email-analysis module, and books the SC-200 exam for the block that starts in May.

- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) — mapping the detection library built in Feb/March
- [LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io/) — incident #2, finished and published
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC specs, read as needed (not a separate course — the theory is small; the practice is the point)

> This month is why the calendar has room in it this year: ATT&CK, a real second incident, and a whole new triage skill (phishing) used to be squeezed into three weeks. They get five now, on purpose — density was the single biggest risk in the old version of this plan.

## Week 3 — 12 to 17 April

The phishing/email-analysis module. Split: 10 h theory and hands-on, 5 h PyTriage extension.

Day | Work through
:-- | :--
Mon 12 | SPF/DKIM/DMARC theory: what each mechanism actually verifies, how a receiving server checks it, what pass/fail/none/softfail mean in a header.
Tue 13 | Email header anatomy: the `Received` chain read bottom-up (origin to inbox), `Return-Path`/`Reply-To`/`From` mismatches, the classic spoofing tells.
Wed 14 | Hands-on: 2 real phishing samples from [malware-traffic-analysis.net](https://www.malware-traffic-analysis.net/), headers analyzed manually — auth results, mismatches, IOC extraction by hand.
Thu 15 | Sandbox analysis: submit one sample to Any.Run or Hybrid Analysis (free tier), read the detonation report, extract IOCs (URLs, hashes, C2 domains) into your notes.
Fri 16 | Extend the **PyTriage CLI** (built [November](2026-11.md)) with `.eml` parsing: header extraction, SPF/DKIM/DMARC verdict parsing, automatic IOC extraction, JSON output.
Sat 17 | Run PyTriage's new `.eml` mode against 2 more samples. **Blog post: phishing triage playbook** — headers, auth checks, sandboxing, IOC extraction, one full worked example end to end.

**Done by Sunday**
- [ ] SPF/DKIM/DMARC and header-spoofing tells written up in your own words

## Rules for a week that goes wrong

- **The phishing week (week 3) does not get cut.** It is the newest skill this plan adds and the one most SOC L1 postings name explicitly under "email security."
- **The SC-200 exam gets booked this month, not next.** The date is the commitment device; May/June is what the date is for.
- **The incident report and the ATT&CK mapping are deliverables, not bonuses.** They prove the triage vocabulary and the detection library are not just vocabulary.
